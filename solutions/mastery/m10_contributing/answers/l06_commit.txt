vars: keep the character after a lone `$`

expand() dropped the character that follows a `$` when the `$` starts
neither a variable (`${NAME}`) nor an escape (`$$`). A step argument
such as `coffee $3` became `coffee $`, so every price in a generated
file lost its number.

The code took the next character out of the iterator before it knew
whether that character belonged to the `$`. Look at it first, and take
it only for `${` and `$$`. A lone `$` now stays as it is and the next
character is handled by the next round of the loop, as the
documentation of expand() says.

Fixes #14
