# Hint directory

[Course overview](README.md)

Open only the unit and the lesson you are working on. Try the first hint before you reveal the next. After you have written your own explanation in the unit's `NOTES.md`, compare it with *Check your explanation*. A hint file follows the unit's study order, which is not always the order of the file numbers: the notes below say where a later-numbered lesson comes before the capstone.

## No hints, on purpose

The four [checkpoints](checkpoints/README.md) and the [final assessment](capstones/final_assessment/README.md) have no hints. They measure what you can do alone; their rubrics tell you what to review afterwards.
